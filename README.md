# 🏦 Bank Customer Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes bank customer data using **SQL Server, Python, and Power BI** to generate business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

A retail bank wants to better understand its customers by analyzing customer demographics, balances, and activity. The objective is to identify customer patterns and provide recommendations that can support business decisions.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains customer information including:

Source: [Bank Customer Churn Dataset]([https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shrutimechlearn/churn-modelling](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/louishgy/churn-modelling?select=Churn_Modelling.csv))

- Customer ID
- Credit Score
- Geography
- Gender
- Age
- Balance
- Estimated Salary
- Active Member Status
- Customer Exit Status

## Key Business Insights

- Male customers make up the majority of the customer base.
- France has the highest number of active customers compared with Germany and Spain.
- Customer balances vary significantly across countries.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Launch engagement campaigns targeting inactive customers to improve retention.
- Investigate the reasons behind the lower customer base in Germany and Spain to identify growth opportunities.
- Design retention strategies for high-value customers.
